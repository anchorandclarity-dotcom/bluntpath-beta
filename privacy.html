Privacy Policy

Last updated: [08/10/2026]

This policy explains what personal data Bluntpath collects, why, and what your rights are. It's written in plain English wherever possible.

Who we are

Bluntpath is operated by Linda Akpojaro, trading as a sole trader in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of UK data protection law, Linda Akpojaro is the "data controller" responsible for your personal data.

Contact: bluntpath@gmail.com

What data we collect

When you use the Discovery and React & Refine forms, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your written answers describing your situation, goals, and what you've already tried
  • Short answers about your available time, money, and personal circumstances (for example, whether anything limits your available time or energy, please don’t include sensitive details such as health information.)
  • Your reactions to the draft directions we give you

When you pay for a report, payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details — Stripe processes that directly under its own privacy policy.

Why we collect it

We use your answers for one purpose: to generate your personalised report, and to let you refine it. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

The legal basis for this processing is that it's necessary to perform the contract you enter into when you pay for a report (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

Who we share it with

To actually deliver the service, your answers are shared with a small number of companies that process data on our behalf ("processors"), strictly to do the specific job described:

  • Anthropic (the company behind Claude, the AI model) — receives your answers in order to generate the written content of your report.
  • Resend — receives your name, email, and report content in order to send it to you by email.
  • Netlify — hosts the website and the backend code that handles your submission. Netlify processes data only as needed to run the site.
  • Stripe — processes your payment directly; we never see your full card details.

Some of these companies are based outside the UK/EEA (including the US). Where that's the case, they provide appropriate safeguards for international data transfers under their own standard contractual terms.

We do not share your data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

We don't keep a database of your answers on our own servers. Your answers exist temporarily in your browser while you complete the form, and then as part of the report sent to your email address (and, for quality review, a copy sent to our own review inbox). Our email provider (Resend) and hosting provider (Netlify) retain standard operational logs for a limited period as part of running their services — we don't control the exact retention period of these underlying logs, but we don't ask them to keep your data any longer than their normal defaults.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Object to how we're using your data
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you think we've handled your data wrongly

To exercise any of these, email bluntpath@gmail.com. We'll respond within one month.

Cookies and tracking

Bluntpath does not currently use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. If this changes in future, this policy will be updated and, where required, we'll ask for your consent first.

Children

Bluntpath is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
