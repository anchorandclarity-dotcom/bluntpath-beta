Your legal right to cancel

Under UK law (the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013), when you buy digital content online, you normally have 14 days to change your mind and get a refund.

However, this right does not apply once we've started providing the digital content, if you've agreed to that happening and acknowledged that you'll lose your cancellation right by doing so.

That's exactly what happens here: before your report is generated, you'll be asked to confirm that you want your report created straight away, and that you understand this means you give up your 14-day cancellation right once it's been generated.

Our refund policy

Because every report is generated specifically for you based on your own answers, and can't be "returned" or resold, we do not offer refunds once your report has been generated, except in the situations below.

When we will refund you

  • If you paid but a technical fault on our end meant no report was ever generated for you
  • If you were charged in error (for example, charged twice for the same report)

In these cases, email lindaakpojaro@gmail.com with your reference number and we'll sort it out.

When we won't refund you

  • You've changed your mind after receiving your report
  • You disagree with the direction the report recommends
  • The report honestly concludes there isn't enough evidence yet for a confident recommendation — this is a genuine, intended outcome of the diagnostic, not a fault
  • You didn't provide enough detail in your answers for a useful report
